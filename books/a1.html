telc Deutsch A1

The beginner book: sounds, survival phrases, articles, present tense, café & shop dialogues, and exam practice.

Who: absolute beginners or false beginners. Goal: pass telc A1 and build habits for A2.

1 · How to use this A1 book

Work with the website: 8-week plan, quizzes, listening voice, writing tasks. The book is for tables and memory. Speak out loud every day — even 5 minutes.

  • Week 1–2: greetings, sein/haben, articles
  • Week 3–4: present tense, questions, café German
  • Week 5–6: accusative, modals, shopping
  • Week 7–8: mocks + weak spots

Daily minimum: 20 vocab cards with articles · one grammar quiz to 80% · 5 minutes speaking.

2 · telc A1 exam map

Parts typically include reading short notices, listening to announcements/dialogues, short writing, and a brief oral. Exact timing varies by centre — always check your invitation.

  • Lesen: signs, emails, ads — match and choose
  • Hören: short texts; some played once
  • Schreiben: forms, messages, short notes
  • Sprechen: introduce yourself, ask/answer, simple planning

Bring passport/ID. Write clearly on the answer sheet. Guess intelligently — never leave blanks if unsure.

3 · Sounds, spelling, survival phrases

ä ö ü ß. W ≈ English v. Z ≈ ts. ich-Laut vs ach-Laut.

Must phrases

  • Guten Tag / Hallo / Tschüss
  • Wie bitte? / Langsamer bitte.
  • Ich verstehe das nicht.
  • Sprechen Sie Englisch? — better: Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?
  • Danke / Bitte / Entschuldigung

4 · Core A1 grammar

sein / haben

ich bin, du bist, er ist, wir sind… · ich habe, du hast, er hat…

Articles

der / die / das · ein / eine · Plural often die. Learn noun + article as one chunk.

Accusative

Masculine: der→den, ein→einen. Ich möchte einen Kaffee.

Present + word order

Verb second: Ich wohne in Berlin. Question: Wohnst du in Berlin?

kein / nicht

kein before nouns; nicht for verbs/adjectives.

Modals

können, müssen, wollen, möchten + infinitive at end.

5 · A1 vocabulary themes

Use the website flashcards. Themes: greetings, people, numbers/time, home, food, daily verbs, travel, work/school, health, shopping, adjectives.

Golden rule: never write a noun without its article. der Bahnhof, die Wohnung, das Ticket.

6 · Reading, listening, writing, speaking

Lesen

Read the question first. Look for synonyms (geschlossen ≈ not open).

Hören

Read the statement first. Listen for numbers, negation, times.

Schreiben

Greeting → points → closing. Keep sentences short and correct.

Sprechen

Memorise a 20-second intro. Ask one question back every time.

7 · Practice checklist

  • Two Lesen papers on the site at 80%+
  • All Hören sets with German voice / then official audio
  • Six writing tasks from memory
  • Intro + 6 questions without notes
  • Mocks A and B under time

8 · A1 appendix

Numbers 0–100

Practice phone numbers aloud. 21 = einundzwanzig (ones before tens).

Days

Montag–Sonntag. am Montag. am Wochenende.

Exam day

ID, pencils, water, arrive early. Breathe. Short correct German beats long broken German.
