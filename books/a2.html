Who: solid A1 or weak A2. Goal: pass telc A2 and arrive ready for B1.

1 · Eight weeks to A2

Pair this book with the website plan. Target 90 minutes most days.

  • Weeks 1–2: Perfekt + dative
  • Weeks 3–4: connectors + separable verbs
  • Weeks 5–6: adjectives, work/travel vocab, letters
  • Weeks 7–8: mocks + official audio

2 · telc A2 exam map

Longer than A1: more reading, denser listening, multi-point writing, structured speaking.

  • Lesen: notices, emails, ads
  • Sprachbausteine-style grammar gaps appear in many practice packs
  • Hören: announcements + dialogues
  • Schreiben: informal + simple formal messages with Leitpunkte
  • Sprechen: intro, topic, simple planning

3 · Perfekt deeply

Spoken past. haben vs sein. Regular ge-t vs irregular ge-en. Separable: aufgestanden.

Write 10 true sentences about yesterday every day for two weeks.

4 · Accusative + dative

Akk: den/einen. Dat: dem/einem, der/einer, den (+n on many nouns in plural).

Prepositions: für/ohne/durch/um (Akk); mit/zu/von/aus/nach/bei (Dat); Wechsel verbs of place.

5 · Connectors & word order

weil/dass/wenn/als → verb end. denn/deshalb/dann → verb second.

Time–Manner–Place is a helpful default, not a prison.

6 · A2 vocabulary

Themes on the site: people, home, daily, food, work, health, travel, services, opinions, media, verbs, connectors.

Collocations: eine Frage stellen, Sport treiben, einen Termin vereinbaren.

7 · Skills & templates

Letter skeleton (du)

Hallo/Liebe … · thanks/reason · four points · Viele Grüße

Letter skeleton (Sie)

Sehr geehrte … · reason · Könnten Sie bitte … · Mit freundlichen Grüßen

Speaking

Meiner Meinung nach… Zum Beispiel… Allerdings… Und du?

8 · Mocks & appendix

Do mocks A–C on the site. Then one official telc A2 sample with real audio.
