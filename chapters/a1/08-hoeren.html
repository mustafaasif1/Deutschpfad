Chapter 7

Hören — listening under exam rules

You cannot read your way to listening points. You can still prepare the task. The website exam sitting plays German audio with reading time, once/twice rules, and an announcer — like telc. In week 8 add the official MP3 for room acoustics.

Before the recording starts

  1. Read every statement. Underline the trap word: time, platform, nicht / nur / schon / erst.
  2. Predict: station, shop, voicemail, café?
  3. Pencil first; copy to the Antwortbogen after the last part.
Once means once If Teil 1 plays once, do not wait for a second chance. Catch the one fact. There is a short pause — look at the next statement, do not mourn the last one.

What to hunt

A number flies byWrite digits immediately. 14 vs 40 (vierzehn / vierzig), 13/30, 16/60
The last sentence cancels the firstThe last relevant fact wins: open on Monday… closed next week

Drill — cover the answers, then check

1 „Der Zug nach Köln fährt von Gleis 3.“ — Statement: Gleis 5. Falsch

2 „Das Café ist heute bis 18 Uhr geöffnet.“ — Statement: closes at 18. Richtig

3 „Frau Becker kommt um 10 Uhr, nicht um 9.“ — Statement: she comes at 9. Falsch

4 „Morgen fällt der Deutschkurs aus.“ — Statement: the course is tomorrow. Falsch

5 „Hallo Tim, ich bin 10 Minuten zu spät. Warte bitte im Café.“ — Statement: Tim should wait in the café. Richtig
