Parts typically include reading short notices, listening to announcements/dialogues, short writing, and a brief oral. Exact timing varies by centre — always check your invitation.

  • Lesen: signs, emails, ads — match and choose
  • Hören: short texts; some played once
  • Schreiben: forms, messages, short notes
  • Sprechen: introduce yourself, ask/answer, simple planning

Bring passport/ID. Write clearly on the answer sheet. Guess intelligently — never leave blanks if unsure.
