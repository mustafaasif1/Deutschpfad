Chapter 1

How to use this book · 8-week plan

Who this is for

You are starting German, or you know a handful of words and freeze in a shop. A1 is survival: name, country, job, time, food, tickets, a doctor’s waiting room. It is not essays and it is not B1 letters.

CEFR A1 in one sentence: you can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases, introduce yourself, and interact if the other person talks slowly and clearly.

How to study

  1. Read a grammar section once for understanding (English is here on purpose).
  2. Copy the tables by hand. der Tisch, not Tisch.
  3. Tap the speaker on the website next to every German word, then say it yourself.
  4. 20–25 vocab cards a day with the article.
  5. Write one short message every two days from Chapter 8, then check the model.
  6. Speak out loud every day — even five minutes. Record your intro until you can do it with the paper covered.
  7. From week 4: one Lesen or Hören set. Guessing is allowed. Blank answers are wasted — there is no penalty for wrong answers.
Do not Do not binge B1 YouTube. Do not skip speaking because you feel shy. The oral is a group performance: you must say something. Train your mouth, not only your eyes.
