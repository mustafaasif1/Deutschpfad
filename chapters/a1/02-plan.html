1 · How to use this A1 book

Work with the website: 8-week plan, quizzes, listening voice, writing tasks. The book is for tables and memory. Speak out loud every day — even 5 minutes.

  • Week 1–2: greetings, sein/haben, articles
  • Week 3–4: present tense, questions, café German
  • Week 5–6: accusative, modals, shopping
  • Week 7–8: mocks + weak spots

Daily minimum: 20 vocab cards with articles · one grammar quiz to 80% · 5 minutes speaking.
