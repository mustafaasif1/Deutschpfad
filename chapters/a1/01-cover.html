telc Deutsch A1

The beginner book: sounds, survival dialogues, articles, present tense, café and shop German, and the exam map.

Who: absolute beginners or false beginners. Goal: pass telc Deutsch A1 (Start Deutsch 1) with a comfortable score, and leave with habits that make A2 easier.

This exam is: adult telc Deutsch A1 / Start Deutsch 1 — forms, ~30-word notes, group speaking, no prep room.

This exam is not: Goethe modular A1 as a separate product, A1 für Zuwanderer orientation extras, or anything B1-shaped (no 100-word letter, no 20-minute oral prep, no Sprachbausteine paper).

What this book is: the tables, scripts, and methods you can memorise so the booklet feels familiar. Grey boxes = say this until it is automatic. Work it with the website: quizzes, speaker on every word, Hören sittings, hide-model writing.

What this book cannot replace: real exam-room audio. In weeks 7–8 download the official telc A1 sample and its MP3. Speaking still needs a mouth — yours, recorded on a phone, or a partner.

Original teaching material. Not affiliated with telc gGmbH or the Goethe-Institut. No pass guarantee.

The deal If you can (1) greet and introduce yourself for 20 seconds, (2) use sein/haben and der/die/das without thinking, (3) order in a café and ask a price, (4) fill a form and write a 30-word message with greeting and closing, (5) catch times and negation in short audio — you can pass. Pretty German is not required. Short correct German is. Aim 80% in this book and on the website so exam day feels slow.
How to read a page Bold grey German = say it aloud. Italic English under it = meaning, not a second sentence to learn. Blue MEMORISE boxes = copy by hand. Orange Danger = the trap that steals marks. Green Trick = the shortcut examiners reward.
