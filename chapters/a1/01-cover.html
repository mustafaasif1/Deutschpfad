telc Deutsch A1

The beginner book: sounds, survival dialogues, articles, present tense, café and shop German, and the exam map.

Who: absolute beginners or false beginners. Goal: pass telc Deutsch A1 and leave with habits that make A2 easier.

What this book is: the tables, scripts, and methods you can memorise so the exam feels familiar. Work it with the website: quizzes, flashcards with a speaker on every word, Hören exam sittings, writing tasks.

What this book cannot replace: real exam-room audio. In weeks 7–8 download the official telc A1 sample and its MP3. Speaking still needs a mouth — yours, recorded on a phone, or a partner.

The deal If you can (1) greet and introduce yourself for 20 seconds, (2) use sein/haben and der/die/das without thinking, (3) order in a café and ask a price, (4) fill a form and write a 30-word message with greeting and closing, (5) catch times and negation in short audio — you can pass. Pretty German is not required. Short correct German is.
