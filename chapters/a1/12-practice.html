Chapter 11

Practice checklist

Do this on the website. Tick only what you can do with the book closed. Pass is about 60%. Train to 80% so the real paper feels slow.

  • All grammar quizzes at 80%+ (sein/haben, articles, present, questions, kein/nicht, accusative, dative, connectors, modals, prepositions).
  • Every official topic ticked only when you can say the chunks without English notes.
  • All Lesen papers at 80%+.
  • All Hören papers in exam sitting (once/twice rules), then trap paper 4 (14 vs 40, Gleis, halb acht). Weeks 7–8: official telc A1 MP3.
  • Eight writing tasks from memory (form + short message). Greeting and closing every time. Model locked until you finish.
  • Six oral runs: intro + new cards + one plan. No 90-second B1 speech.
  • Timed mocks A and B, then C–G if you still have time. Pause only between parts.
Exam week Sleep. ID. Pencils. One last intro in the bathroom mirror. Do not cram new grammar the night before — drill weak vocab and your letter skeleton.

Sprachbausteine on the site is extra grammar practice. It is not a separate official A1 subtest. Use it if articles and word order still wobble.
