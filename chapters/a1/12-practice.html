Chapter 11

Practice checklist

Do this on the website. Tick only what you can do with the book closed.

  • All grammar quizzes at 80%+ (sein/haben, articles, present, questions, kein/nicht, accusative, modals, prepositions).
  • Every vocab topic: speaker + type-the-German quiz.
  • All Lesen papers at 80%+.
  • All Hören papers in exam sitting (once/twice rules). Then weeks 7–8: official telc A1 MP3.
  • Six writing tasks from memory (form + short message). Greeting and closing every time.
  • Intro + six questions without notes. One shop and one café roleplay recorded.
  • Timed mocks A and B, then C–F if you still have time. Pause only between parts.
Exam week Sleep. ID. Pencils. One last intro in the bathroom mirror. Do not cram new grammar the night before — drill weak vocab and your letter skeleton.

Sprachbausteine on the site is extra grammar practice. It is not a separate official A1 subtest. Use it if articles and word order still wobble.
