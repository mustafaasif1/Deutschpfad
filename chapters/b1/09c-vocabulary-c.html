False friends

bekommen = receive, not become (werden). gift in German is poison; gift is das Geschenk. also = therefore, not also (auch). eventuell = possibly, not eventually. aktuell = current, not actual (eigentlich / tatsächlich). sensibel = sensitive, not sensible (vernünftig). das Gymnasium is a school, not a gym (das Fitnessstudio). die Highschool is not a German word you need; use Schulform. werben = advertise, not to become.
