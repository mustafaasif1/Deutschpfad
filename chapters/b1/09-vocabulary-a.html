Chapter 9

Vocabulary you must own

Do not learn isolated English translations. Learn article + word + plural + one chunk. Example: die Miete, die Mieten — die Miete bezahlen. This chapter is the exam’s world: home, work, health, travel, money, courses, complaints, plans, opinions. Tick 20–25 entries a day. If you know every table here, you have enough words to pass.

How to memorise Cover the English. Say the German aloud with the article. Then make one sentence. Then reverse: look at English, produce German. Sleep, then test again tomorrow. Words you miss three times go on a “nasty list” in the back of your notebook.
