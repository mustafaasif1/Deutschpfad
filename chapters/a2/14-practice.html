This book teaches the method. The Deutschpfad website is where you perform it under a clock. Do not invent a third resource pile. Use the list below in order. Tick in pencil.

Website map (open these, do not wander)

  • 8-week plan on the A2 home — same spine as Chapter 1 of this book.
  • Grammar lessons + drills: Perfekt, dative, connectors, separable, adjectives. Redo anything under 80%.
  • Vocab trainer: people, home, work, health, travel, shopping. Always with article.
  • Lesen papers inside the mocks and as separate reading sets.
  • Hören papers in exam mode — all of them. Teil 1 once if the player allows; train that discipline even when you could rewind.
  • Sprachbausteine drills — extra gap practice. Useful even if your centre’s adult paper has no separate SB subtest. Grammar still lives in Lesen and Schreiben.
  • Schreiben tasks with the timer: invite, course, problem, shop, visit. Aim 60–80 words, all points.
  • Sprechen prompts — Teil 1 questions, Teil 2 topics, Teil 3 planning. Use the Redemittel from Chapter 11.
  • Six mocks A–F — written half as one sitting each. Mock A in week 7; B and C in week 8; D–F if you have time or as a retake of weak skills.
Mock sitting rules Phone in another room. Hören first (about 20 min), Lesen (30), Schreiben (30). No dictionary. Transfer answers if the site shows an answer sheet. Then mark. Write your three weakest error types in this book’s Chapter 12 table. Fix those before the next mock — do not just “do another mock” as entertainment.

Weeks 7–8 — official telc audio

Download a free official telc Deutsch A2 Übungstest and its MP3s from telc’s website (search “telc Deutsch A2 mock examination” or use the link on the Deutschpfad week-8 card). Sit it once under exam time. Then — and this is the part people skip — sit the same audio again two days later and notice how Teil 1 feels slower. Familiar official audio is worth more than five random YouTube clips.

Speaking practice card (print this page or copy)

  1. Teil 1: 3 minutes. Intro with Freizeit + weil. Two follow-up questions.
  2. Teil 2: 90 seconds on a photo or topic from Chapter 11, then ask the partner.
  3. Teil 3: 5 minutes. Plan a party or a trip. End with Dann sind wir uns einig.

Do this three times in week 7 and twice in week 8. If you have no partner, record, listen, and redo the thin parts.

Week-8 “80% and sleep” list

  • No new grammar. Skim Chapters 3–6 and the appendix only.
  • Redo every website quiz still under 80%.
  • Write three messages from memory (du, Sie, du) in 25 minutes each.
  • One official sample audio. One website mock you have not sat cleanly.
  • Night before: ID, pencils, this book’s exam-day list. Sleep. Do not cram irregulars at 1 a.m.
After you pass A2 is a platform. Keep Perfekt and weil boringly easy. That is the start of B1 — not Konjunktiv II videos the next morning.
