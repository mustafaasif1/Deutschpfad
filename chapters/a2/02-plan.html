Chapter 1

How to use this book · 8-week plan

Who this is for

You can already do A1 survival: say who you are, order in a café, ask the price, talk about your day in the present tense, and write a short form. You still freeze when the story moves to yesterday. You say Ich gehe Bahnhof instead of Ich gehe zum Bahnhof. You know weil means “because” but you leave the verb in the English place. That is normal. A2 is the level where German starts to connect: past + reason + place.

CEFR A2 in one sentence: you can handle simple everyday tasks, describe your background and surroundings in simple terms, and write a short connected message about familiar things. telc Deutsch A2 tests exactly that — not B1 complaint letters, not Konjunktiv II production, not long relative-clause essays.

How to study (do this, not “read passively”)

  1. Read a grammar section once for understanding (English explanations are here on purpose).
  2. Copy the MEMORISE tables by hand into a notebook. Hand memory beats highlighting.
  3. Say every example aloud. A2 speaking rewards mouths that have already said the sentence.
  4. Learn vocabulary as article + noun. Never learn Wohnung — learn die Wohnung. Never learn Arzt — learn der Arzt.
  5. Write ten true Perfekt sentences about yesterday every day in weeks 1–2. Then write one short message every two days from Chapter 10 with the model covered.
  6. Record yourself doing the Teil 1 intro and one Teil 2 topic, three times a week from week 4.
  7. From week 5: timed reading and listening on the website. Guessing is allowed. Blank answers are wasted — there is no penalty for wrong answers.
High marks path Six pair oral runs (weil + Einverstanden in Teil 3). Eight hide-model letters (one du, one Sie). Hören trap paper 5 (14 vs 40, Gleis, erst/schon). Then the official telc A2 MP3. Website Exam gym has the same card.
Do not Do not start with B1 YouTube “Konjunktiv II in 10 minutes”. Do not collect ten apps. Do not only read. The exam is a performance: you must write a connected message in about 30 minutes and talk to a stranger in German for about 15. Train that.

What “memorise” means here

You do not need 5,000 words. You need about 800–1,200 active words (with gender) plus 40–50 irregular participles plus a handful of connectors plus the letter and speaking formulas. That is this book. If a word is in a MEMORISE box or in Chapter 7, it is exam-useful. Learn those before rare synonyms.

Trick — the 80/20 of A2 Examiners reward clear, connected, appropriate language. A correct weil-clause, a clean Perfekt, and a polite Könnten Sie bitte…? beat a broken relative clause or a fancy word you cannot spell. Stay at clean A2.

What “connected A2” looks like

Too weak (A1 list): Ich gehe Arzt. Es geht nicht gut. Ich habe Kopf.

A2 target: Gestern bin ich zum Arzt gegangen, weil ich Kopfschmerzen hatte. Der Arzt war freundlich. Danach habe ich zu Hause Tee getrunken.

Unnecessary (B1+): Nachdem ich den Facharzt konsultiert hatte, ließ ich weitere Untersuchungen durchführen, obwohl ich eigentlich hätte arbeiten sollen.

The middle paragraph is the exam. Past + reason + a simple next step. That is enough.
