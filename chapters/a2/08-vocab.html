Chapter 7

Vocabulary by topic — the words you must own

telc A2 recycles the same rooms of life: a flat, a job or course, a doctor, a trip, a shop, other people. You do not need rare newspaper words. You need these nouns with their article so dative and accusative have somewhere to land.

Golden rule Never learn a naked noun. Learn die Wohnung, not “Wohnung”. Learn der Arzt, not “Arzt”. Say the article every time you test yourself. If you only know the English, you will write Ich gehe Arzt forever.

How to learn: 20 words a day, article + German + English, then three spoken sentences. Copy each table by hand once. The website quizzes the same themes — use them after you have written the list, not instead of writing it.

Gender patterns that save you -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -tion are almost always die: die Wohnung, die Gesundheit, die Freundlichkeit, die Nachbarschaft, die Reservierung, die Information. Jobs in -er are often der (Lehrer, Fahrer); add -in for women. Diminutives -chen are das. When you guess, guess — then check once and write the article in ink.
