telc Deutsch A2

The complete builder book: Perfekt, dative, connectors, letters, speaking scripts, and the exam map.

Written for a learner who can already survive A1 — shop, introduce yourself, talk about today in the present — and who now needs connected past, dative, and a real telc A2 performance.

What this book is: everything you must know and can memorise so the exam feels familiar: haben vs sein, articles in three cases, weil/dass/wenn, separable verbs, topic vocabulary, a 60–80 word message, pair-speaking scripts, and how each written and oral part actually looks.

What this book cannot replace: real listening audio. After you learn the method here, sit the website mocks with a timer, then download a free official telc A2 sample and its MP3s. Speaking still needs a partner or a phone recording of yourself.

Study plan inside: 8 weeks from A1 survival to exam-ready. Read, highlight, memorise the grey boxes, then write and speak every day.

The deal If you can (1) tell last weekend in Perfekt without ich habe gegangen, (2) use dative after mit / zu / helfen / gefallen, (3) put the verb last after weil / dass / wenn, (4) write a connected 60–80 word message covering every guiding point, and (5) talk to a partner for 15 minutes using the scripts — you can pass. Perfection is not required. About 60% in the written half and 60% in the oral half is the pass line.
