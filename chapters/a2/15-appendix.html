Exam-day card

  • Passport or national ID — name matches the registration.
  • Pencils (HB/2B), eraser, pen if the centre wants ink for Schreiben.
  • Watch without smart functions if phones stay outside.
  • Arrive early. Toilets before Hören. Water if allowed.
  • Hören: read the statement first. Teil 1 often once. Hunt numbers / nicht / nur.
  • Lesen: questions first. Synonyms. Transfer to the Antwortbogen with minutes left.
  • Schreiben: circle du or Sie. Tick every Leitpunkt. 60–80 words. Greeting + close. One weil, one Perfekt if the story is past.
  • Sprechen: greet the partner. Intro + Freizeit + weil. Teil 2 spine. Teil 3: agree out loud.
  • Always answer. No minus marking.
  • If you miss an instruction: Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?
The deal, one last time Clean Perfekt, dative after mit/zu/helfen, verb last after weil, a connected 60–80 word message, and 15 minutes of pair talk. That is telc Deutsch A2. You do not need B1 machinery. You need this book, the six website mocks, and one official audio sitting.
